About Us

We started because these conversations matter.

Open Journal Youth was founded by a group of high school students who recognised that the conversations around mental health, female hygiene, and eating disorders were happening in whispers — or not at all.

We believe that young women deserve spaces to speak openly, access resources without shame, and build community around shared experiences. That's why we created Open Journal.

Our three focus areas

All resources

Mental Health

Breaking the stigma around mental health in young women — through community conversations, peer support, and connecting students with professional resources.

Explore this area
🌿

Female Hygiene

Advocating for period equity and hygiene access in schools and communities — running drives, education campaigns, and working to eliminate period poverty.

Explore this area
🌸

Eating Disorder Awareness

Creating compassionate, evidence-based awareness around eating disorders — promoting body acceptance, safe conversations, and pathways to support.
